# "All Models Are Wrong, But Some Are Useful" 💬

## The Quote

This line is commonly attributed to statistician George Box. The usual meaning is simple: models simplify reality, so they are never perfectly true, but they can still help us think, predict, decide, and test.

## Why It Has Teeth

The quote attacks two bad habits at once.

First, it attacks naive belief: "The model says it, so it must be true."

Second, it attacks lazy dismissal: "The model is imperfect, so it is useless."

The better question is: useful for what?

## Where It Helps

- Forecasting
- Budgets
- Software estimates
- Economic theories
- Personality frameworks
- Medical risk scores
- Climate models
- Product analytics

Every model has a job. Judge it against the job, not against omniscience.

## Why It Is Interesting

The quote is a permission slip for practical thinking. It lets you use simplified tools without worshipping them.

A model is a lantern, not the sun.
