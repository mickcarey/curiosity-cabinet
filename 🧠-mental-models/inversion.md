# Inversion: Solve It Backwards 🧠

## The Model

Inversion means asking the problem backwards.

Instead of "How do I succeed?", ask "How would I guarantee failure?" Then avoid those causes with suspicious enthusiasm.

It is useful because the path to success is often foggy, but the path to disaster has signposts, potholes, and sometimes a gift shop.

## How To Use It

- Want a better project? Ask what would make it late, brittle, confusing, or unloved.
- Want better health? Ask what would reliably wreck sleep, movement, food, and mood.
- Want better writing? Ask what would make the piece boring, vague, padded, or spineless.
- Want better learning? Ask what would create the illusion of progress without recall.

## Why It Works

Humans are good at spotting hazards when invited directly. Inversion turns anxiety into a checklist.

It also fights wishful thinking. A goal can become a poster. A failure mode becomes a doorstop.
