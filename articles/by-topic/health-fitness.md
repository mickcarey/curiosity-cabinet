# 🏃 Health & Fitness

*The curious, detailed, occasionally humbling exploration of the human body in motion*

A collection of deep-dive articles on what it actually means to move well, build real strength, and develop the engine for endurance. Written with the conviction that understanding the science makes the practice far more interesting — and effective.

---

## The Series

### [The Art of Not Being a Stiff Board: Stretching, Flexibility & Mobility](health-fitness-flexibility.md)

Everything you need to know about flexibility vs mobility (not the same thing), how the nervous system governs range of motion, the five types of stretching, and how to build a daily practice that actually works. Covers muscle spindles, the Golgi tendon organ, PNF stretching, and why your hips probably hoard more restrictions than a bureaucrat with a filing cabinet.

### [The Magnificent Machine: Human Anatomy for Movement & Injury Prevention](health-fitness-anatomy.md)

A practical tour of your own architecture. The joint-by-joint model, spinal mechanics, hip complex anatomy, the knee's structural trade-offs, shoulder stability, and the real core (hint: it's more than a six-pack). Explains why lower back pain usually isn't a lower back problem, and traces the anatomical roots of the injuries athletes encounter most.

### [Gravity Is Your Barbell: Progressions & Leverage in Bodyweight Training](health-fitness-bodyweight.md)

Using physics and leverage to build elite strength without a gym. From push-up to planche, from inverted row to front lever, from bodyweight squat to pistol — the complete progression framework for the push, pull, squat, hinge, and core patterns. Includes isometric training, the principle of progressive overload in bodyweight context, and why elite gymnasts have some of the most remarkable strength-to-weight ratios in sport.

### [The Long Game: Endurance Fitness for Running, Surfing & Not Giving Up](health-fitness-endurance.md)

Energy systems, cardiovascular adaptation, the polarised training model, lactate threshold, VO₂ max, and the science of fatigue. Special sections on surfing-specific fitness, CO2 tolerance training for breath-hold and wipeouts, long-distance running development, and running economy. Also: the mental dimension of endurance, and why some of what you experience as exhaustion is negotiable.
