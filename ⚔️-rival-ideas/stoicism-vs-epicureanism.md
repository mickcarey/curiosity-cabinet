# Stoicism vs Epicureanism: Calm Under Two Different Roofs ⚔️

## The Rivalry

Stoicism and Epicureanism both ask: how do you live without being dragged around by fear, appetite, status, and disaster?

Their answers rhyme, but they do not match.

Stoicism says peace comes from virtue, reason, and accepting what is outside your control. Epicureanism says peace comes from modest pleasures, friendship, clear thinking, and removing unnecessary fears, especially fear of death and divine punishment.

## The Sharp Contrast

- **Stoicism:** Be excellent in your character, even when fortune behaves like a spilled toolbox.
- **Epicureanism:** Want less, fear less, enjoy simple goods, and stop feeding imaginary disasters.
- **Stoicism:** The world is ordered, and your job is to meet it with disciplined judgment.
- **Epicureanism:** The world is atoms and void, and your job is to build a tranquil life inside it.

## Why It Is Interesting

Modern culture often turns Stoicism into productivity armour and Epicureanism into fancy snacks. Both are unfair reductions.

The deeper contrast is better: Stoicism trains dignity under pressure; Epicureanism trains freedom from needless hunger. One is a fortress. The other is a garden with excellent boundaries.
