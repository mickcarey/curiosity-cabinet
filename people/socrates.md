# Socrates 🗣️
*The OG Philosopher Who Never Wrote Anything Down*

## The Walking Question Mark of Athens

Born around 470 BCE in Athens, Socrates was basically the ancient Greek version of that friend who answers every question with another question. And he did it SO WELL that they made him drink poison for it! 😅☠️

## The Philosophy Bro

Socrates looked like your average Ancient Greek dude – except for his famously ugly face (sources describe him as snub-nosed, bulging-eyed, and generally toad-like). But boy, could this man TALK! He spent his days wandering around Athens, barefoot and probably unwashed, interrogating anyone who claimed to know anything. 🦶

His favorite pastime? The "Socratic Method" – a fancy term for asking you questions until you realize you don't actually know what you're talking about. Imagine going to the market to buy olives and leaving with an existential crisis instead! 🫒➡️😱

## The Professional Annoyer

Socrates claimed a "divine voice" (daimonion) guided him. Was it wisdom from the gods or just really good intuition? Either way, he used it to become Athens' most beloved pain in the neck. His specialty was approaching self-important politicians, priests, and "wise men" and systematically destroying their confidence through questions.

**Classic Socrates Move:**
- Random Athenian: "I'm an expert on justice!"
- Socrates: "Cool! What is justice?"
- Random Athenian: *explains confidently*
- Socrates: *asks 47 follow-up questions*
- Random Athenian: *has a breakdown* "I... I don't know anything anymore!"
- Socrates: "NOW you're getting wise!" 😏

## The World's Worst Student Advisor

Despite never writing anything himself (seriously, not a SINGLE scroll), Socrates became the teacher of some seriously heavy hitters:
- **Plato** 📝 – His #1 fanboy who wrote EVERYTHING down
- **Xenophon** – His other biographer
- **Alcibiades** – Athens' most controversial politician (oops!)

Teaching method: 100% conversations, 0% syllabi. Final exam: Question everything forever.

## The Trial That Went Too Far

In 399 BCE, at age 70, Athens got tired of Socrates' shenanigans and put him on trial for:
1. **Corrupting the youth** (aka making young people question authority)
2. **Not believing in the gods** (he believed in gods, just not the specific approved ones)

His defense strategy? Double down on being annoying! Instead of apologizing, he suggested Athens should give him free meals for life as a reward for his services. The jury was NOT amused. 🙄

Verdict: Guilty. Sentence: Death by hemlock.

## The Death That Launched a Thousand Philosophy Classes

Offered a chance to escape, Socrates refused. Why? Because breaking the law – even an unjust one – would contradict everything he taught about virtue and integrity. So he drank the hemlock poison, spent his last hours discussing the immortality of the soul (as one does), and died surrounded by his weeping students.

Plato wrote about it and made everyone cry for 2,400+ years. Thanks, Plato! 😭

## Fun Facts & Quirks 🎪

- **Married to Xanthippe** – famously described as difficult (though maybe she was just tired of him asking "but WHY?" about every household decision)
- **War Veteran** – Fought in three military campaigns and was reportedly super brave
- **Poverty Pride** – Believed in living simply; owned basically nothing and thought it was great
- **The Barefoot Warrior** – Never wore shoes, even in winter. His students thought he was hardcore
- **Champion Standing-Still-er** – Once stood in the same spot for 24 hours just... thinking
- **Ugliest Philosopher Award** 🏆 – Contemporary sources really didn't hold back on this

## Philosophy's Greatest Hits 💭

- **"The unexamined life is not worth living"** – AKA: Think about your choices!
- **"I know that I know nothing"** – The ultimate humble-brag
- **"To find yourself, think for yourself"** – Stop listening to influencers!
- **"Be kind, for everyone you meet is fighting a hard battle"** – Surprisingly wholesome for a professional arguer

## The Socratic Legacy

Socrates never:
- ❌ Wrote a book
- ❌ Founded a school
- ❌ Had a YouTube channel

But he DID:
- ✅ Invent critical thinking as we know it
- ✅ Create the blueprint for Western philosophy
- ✅ Prove you can be massively influential without an Instagram account
- ✅ Show that asking questions is more powerful than giving answers

## Current Status

Been dead for 2,400 years, but we're STILL talking about him. His "method" is taught in law schools, business schools, and therapy sessions worldwide. Every time someone says "Well, that depends on what you mean by..." – that's Socrates' ghost, still out there asking questions. 👻
