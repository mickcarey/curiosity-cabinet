# Feedback Loops: Thinking In Circles Without Getting Dizzy 🧠

## The Model

A feedback loop happens when a system's output circles back and changes its future input.

Simple cause and effect says: A causes B.

Feedback thinking says: A causes B, then B comes back wearing muddy boots and changes A.

## Two Basic Types

- **Reinforcing loops:** more creates more, or less creates less. Compound interest, viral sharing, panic, trust, distrust.
- **Balancing loops:** the system pushes back toward a target. Thermostats, hunger, queues, budgets, body temperature.

## Why It Is Useful

Feedback loops explain why some problems keep returning after a "fix." The fix may quiet the symptom while strengthening the loop that caused it.

Example: a team under deadline pressure skips documentation. That makes future work harder. Future work then creates more deadline pressure. Congratulations, the loop has found a chair and moved in.

## Why It Is Interesting

Feedback loops are the plot twists of systems. They are why local decisions can create global weirdness.
