## Big Questions

### [Consciousness and the Mind: The Universe Looking Back at Itself](../by-topic/consciousness-and-the-mind.md)
*Original Article • July 2026*

A broad map of subjective experience, selfhood, animal consciousness, machine minds, and the stubborn question of why physical matter ever has an inner view. Keeps the octopus and Chinese Room overlap explicit without redoing those notes.

**The takeaway that stuck:** Intelligence asks whether a system can solve the problem; consciousness asks whether there is someone there for whom the problem appears.

### [How Civilisations Succeed or Fail](../by-topic/how-civilisations-succeed-or-fail.md)
*Original Article • July 2026*

A systems-level look at resilience, legitimacy, state capacity, trust, complexity, institutional decay, and the warning lights that suggest a society is losing adaptive capacity.

**The useful frame:** Civilisations fail when problem-generation outruns problem-solving and enough people stop believing the system is worth maintaining.

### [The Origins and Limits of the Universe](../by-topic/origins-and-limits-of-the-universe.md)
*Original Article • July 2026*

A cosmology primer on the Big Bang, the cosmic microwave background, inflation, dark matter, dark energy, the observable universe, and why "before the Big Bang" might be a broken question.

**The brain-bender:** The Big Bang was not an explosion into space; it was space itself expanding.

### [How Intelligence Really Works](../by-topic/how-intelligence-really-works.md)
*Original Article • July 2026*

A tour through learning, memory, creativity, intuition, bounded rationality, AI, and why clever people can still believe nonsense with orchestral confidence.

**The calibration trick:** The question is not "Do you trust your gut?" It is "What trained the gut?"

### [Designing a Genuinely Good Society](../by-topic/designing-a-genuinely-good-society.md)
*Original Article • July 2026*

A practical anti-utopian framework for balancing freedom, fairness, meaningful work, technology, community, sustainability, and democratic maintenance.

**The core image:** A good society needs a strong floor and open sky.
