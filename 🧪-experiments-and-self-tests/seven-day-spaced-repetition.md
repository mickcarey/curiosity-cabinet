# Seven Days Of Spaced Repetition 🧪

## The Experiment

Pick one tiny skill or memory set and test whether spaced repetition actually helps over a single week.

Good candidates:

- 20 music intervals
- 30 foreign-language words
- 15 keyboard shortcuts
- 10 historical dates
- 12 anatomy terms

The point is not to become a genius in seven days. The point is to see whether repeated retrieval makes the knowledge feel less slippery.

## The Rules

- Day 1: learn the set and test immediately.
- Day 2: test without looking first, then review misses.
- Day 4: test again.
- Day 7: final test.
- Keep the same prompt format each time.
- Record score and subjective effort.

## What To Notice

- Which items became automatic?
- Which items kept escaping?
- Did wrong answers cluster by similarity?
- Did testing feel more useful than rereading?

## Why It Is Interesting

Memory is less like pouring water into a bucket and more like making a trail through grass. Retrieval is the walking. Review is just looking at the field and feeling educational.
